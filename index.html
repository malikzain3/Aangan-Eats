How it works

Tiffin is a subscription. Choose a plan once and your meals arrive on schedule, with no daily re-ordering.

  1. 1

    Pick a plan

    Single, double or triple meal, daily, weekly or monthly.

  2. 2

    Choose your kitchen

    Select a registered home chef near your campus.

  3. 3

    Meals arrive

    Student riders bring your tiffin to the hostel gate.

  4. 4

    Swap when you want

    Request a different dish one day ahead.
